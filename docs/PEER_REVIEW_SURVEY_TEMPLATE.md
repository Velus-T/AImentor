## Rating questions

Use a 1–5 scale: 1 = strongly disagree, 5 = strongly agree.

1. The Science module is easy to understand on first use.
2. Physics, Chemistry and Biology tutors are clearly different.
3. The chat layout is familiar and easy to continue with follow-up questions.
4. Text, gallery image and camera submission are easy to find.
5. AI answers match the selected education level and explanation style.
6. Chemistry formulas and equations are readable.
7. API Settings are understandable without editing code.
8. History search/filter and Bookmark are useful.
9. Adaptive Quiz changes questions while retaining the selected level.
10. Quiz answer options, radio buttons and feedback are easy to read/select.
11. The Study Planner starts from the creation time and produces a realistic plan.
12. Checkboxes, spinners and other options remain clear in light and dark mode.
13. The animated chat background is attractive without distracting from content.
14. The circuit diagram and periodic table support Science learning.
15. The solution is stable enough for normal student use.
